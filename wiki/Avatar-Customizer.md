# Avatar Customizer

## How to Pick Your Avatar

1. In the 3D Studio toolbar, click the **Avatar** button
2. The Avatar Picker dialog opens

## Preset Avatars

| Preset | Default Color | Accessory |
|--------|---------------|-----------|
| Knight | Blue (#3b82f6) | Gold helmet |
| Mage | Purple (#8b5cf6) | Pointed hat |
| Archer | Green (#10b981) | None |
| Rogue | Dark (#1f2937) | None |
| Wizard | Indigo (#1e3a8a) | Pointed hat |
| Robot | Cyan (#06b6d4) | None |

## Customizer

### Body Type
- **Slim** — 85% size, narrower frame
- **Average** — Default size
- **Tall** — 130% size, taller frame

### Color
- 10 preset colors
- Custom color picker (any hex value)

## Preview

The preview section shows your selected avatar with its current settings. Your avatar appears in the 3D scene during play-test mode.

## Avatar in 3D Scene

When you click **Play**, your avatar spawns at the origin (0, 0) in the 3D scene. The avatar is a blocky character with:
- Head (with eyes)
- Body (with belt)
- Arms (left and right)
- Legs (left and right)
- Accessory (hat/helmet for certain presets)

The avatar has an idle animation (gentle bobbing + slight rotation).
