# Templates

All In One Engine includes 5+ starter templates.

## 2D Templates (Pygame)

### Platformer
- Player movement (WASD / Arrow keys)
- Gravity and jumping
- Tilemap collision
- Camera follow with smoothing
- Coyote time + jump buffering

### FPS Shooter
- First-person controls (WASD + mouse)
- Gun model with muzzle flash
- Bullets with collision
- Enemy AI (chase player)
- HP / Score / Ammo HUD
- Pause overlay (ESC)
- Menu overlay (TAB)
- Reload mechanic (R)

### Castle Explorer
- Medieval castle with throne room
- Tower with treasure
- Wall collision
- Guards (enemies)
- Score system
- Stone wall textures

### Multiplayer Arena
- 2-4 player online via socket.io
- Real-time position sync
- Player names
- Connection status indicator
- Grid arena with borders

### Physics Sandbox
- Bouncing balls with gravity
- Wall collisions with restitution
- Ball-ball collisions (elastic)
- Click to spawn new balls
- Up to 50 balls

## 3D Templates (Three.js / Zhitlow)

### 3D Sandbox
- Floor plane + box + sphere + pillar + directional light
- Walk around with WASD + mouse look

### 3D FPS Arena
- Walled arena (4 walls)
- 3 target spheres to shoot
- 2 cover blocks
- Sun light + point light

### 3D Castle
- Keep (central building)
- 4 corner towers (cylinders)
- 4 walls
- Gate
- Flag pole + flag
- Sun light

### 3D Multiplayer Arena
- 4 spawn points (colored)
- 3 cover blocks
- Sun light + blue point light
