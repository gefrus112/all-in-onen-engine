# Publishing

## How to Publish Your Game

### Step 1: Build HTML5

In the 3D Studio or 2D Studio toolbar, click the **Publish** button. In the dialog, click **Build HTML5**. A `game.html` file will be downloaded to your computer.

### Step 2: Pick a Platform

| Platform | Best For | Cost | Revenue |
|----------|----------|------|---------|
| itch.io | Indie games, game jams | Free | Optional (pay-what-you-want) |
| Crazy Games | Web game portal | Free | Revenue share |
| GitHub Pages | Sharing with collaborators | Free | None |
| HTML5 Standalone | Self-hosting | Varies | Yours |

### Step 3: Follow Platform Instructions

Each platform has step-by-step instructions in the Publish dialog. Click a platform card to see the steps.

### Tips for Getting Featured

- Add screenshots and a gameplay video
- Write a clear description and use good tags
- Test on Chrome, Firefox, and Safari
- Add mobile touch controls for broader reach
- Share on social media with #AllInOneEngine
